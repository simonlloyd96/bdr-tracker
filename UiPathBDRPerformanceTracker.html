Weekly targets & scoring

Set the weekly target for each metric and how much it counts toward the weighted score. Changes apply to every BDR and every week.

The weighted score is each metric's attainment (capped at 100%) multiplied by its weight. Weights are normalised, so they don't have to add up to exactly 100.
